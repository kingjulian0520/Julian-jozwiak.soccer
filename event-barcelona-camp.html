Barcelona Camp

November 2021 · Barcelona

After attending the Barcelona Camp in Schaumburg, IL, I was emailed an invite to attend the camp in Spain. For 7 days in November, we had hours and hours of training. We played a few Spanish academies and came out of each game with a win — one of these games even ended 21-0. We stayed with our team of 10 players in one room and built friendships with people from all around the U.S.
